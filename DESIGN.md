# Design

## Direction

Team Directory is a restrained native roster for an internal work context. People and their contact details lead; decoration stays secondary.

## Interface Rules

- Use system typography and native navigation behavior.
- Use neutral semantic surfaces with one blue interaction accent.
- Prefer full-width list rows over card grids.
- Make avatars, names, and email addresses easy to scan.
- Keep controls at least 48 points tall and provide accessible names.
- Support light and dark appearance.
- Loading, error, and empty states explain what is happening and the next action.
